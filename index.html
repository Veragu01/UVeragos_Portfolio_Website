Projects

Scratch Coding Workshops

Once a month I run a coding workshop at New Lynn Library, where we teach kids aged 8 and up to create games in Scratch. Scratch introduces programming through a simple, hands on approach where kids connect 'blocks' of code to build small games reminiscent of 2010s Flash games. I’ve run a handful of workshops so far and plan to continue, with the hope of expanding to other libraries around Auckland.

Status: Ongoing

Radio-Controlled Aircraft

I’m designing, programming, and building a remote-controlled plane from scratch with a collaborator who studies Aerospace Engineering. Our goal is to create a plane with a one-metre wingspan, controlled with an Xbox controller over RF communication. The system uses two ESP32s: one in the controller to send inputs, and another on the plane as its “brain.”

Status: Ongoing
